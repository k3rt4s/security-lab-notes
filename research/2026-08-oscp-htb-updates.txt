# OSCP / HTB Study Updates (checked ~2026-08-19)

Purpose: current-state notes for the training/oscp section - what a 2026 candidate should
know about the exam and the practice platforms. Public prep resources only.

OSCP / PEN-200 EXAM
  No confirmed OSCP/PEN-200 exam-format or syllabus change was found dated inside the
  2026-05-21 .. 2026-08-19 window. Say this plainly - do not imply a change happened. (The
  newest OffSec product update indexed during research was the Jan 2026 update, published
  2026-02-25, which covered an upcoming AI cert, not OSCP format.)

  Current baseline still in effect (effective 2024-11-01, not new in-window):
    - Passing PEN-200 grants both OSCP (lifetime) and OSCP+ (3-year, renewable via CPE/recert).
    - Active Directory set is mandatory, worth 40 points, "assumed compromise" AD start.
    - Bonus points removed. Exam = 23h45m hands-on + 24h report; 70/100 to pass.
  Source: OffSec help center "Changes to the OSCP" (help.offsec.com) - returned 403 to direct
  fetch during research; point structure/date corroborated across secondary summaries. Confirm
  in a browser before relying on exact point values.

    per Machine - fewer spawn/performance issues. Just before the window but operative through it.
  - LetsDefend acquisition content (~2026-07-25): large defensive/DFIR expansion in HTB Academy
    (reported 232+ Blue Team modules, 127 new Sherlocks); some free-tier. In-window.
  - Academy content cadence in-window: 191 defensive modules added ~2026-07-30 (16 Tier 0 +
    175 Tier 1); an "AI Literacy" module on agent security ~2026-08-03.
  - Recent Machines (verify difficulty/topic on the live catalog before recommending for OSCP-
    style prep - these came from a search summary, not a per-machine fetch): DarkZeroReturns
    (Hard, ~07-25), DanglingTree (Medium, ~08-08), TrustFall (Insane, ~08-15).
  Source: HTB changelog (roadmap.hackthebox.com/changelog) and HTB blog. Dates are the verified
  element; exact module wording is paraphrased from a fetch summary.

STUDY ANGLE
  The OSCP exam itself is stable in this window; the movement is on the practice platforms
  (dedicated instances, a large defensive/DFIR content push, and AI-security modules). For a
  PEN-200 candidate the actionable items are: rely on the live TJnull sheet, expect dedicated
  HTB instances, and treat the new blue-team/AI modules as breadth, not OSCP-exam scope.
